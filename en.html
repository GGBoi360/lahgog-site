Hebrew birthdays, calculated right.

A Hebrew-calendar birthday falls on a different Gregorian date every year. Lahgog works it out for you, including the cases everyone gets wrong:

  • Born after sunsetThe Hebrew date had already moved to the next day, and that's the one that counts.
  • Adar I and Adar IIIn a leap year, everyone celebrates in the right month.
  • The 30th of Heshvan or KislevEven in years when the month has no 30th.

Privacy policy

Questions

How much does it cost?

The download is free. Some skins and extras are paid, as shown in the app before any purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account everything works on your phone. An account is needed only for friends, chats, the cloud copy and AI.

Is there an iPhone version?

Lahgog is available for Android for now.

What does the AI see?

The AI runs only when you ask, and the app explains what is sent before the first use. You can turn it off in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I rely on the Hebrew dates?

They follow the standard Hebrew calendar, Adar I and Adar II included. For dates with religious meaning, such as a memorial or a bar mitzvah, check with an authoritative source.

Is the app in English?

Yes. The app is Hebrew first, and you can switch it to English in Settings.
