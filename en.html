Privacy policy

Questions

How much does it cost?

The download is free. Some skins and extras are paid, as shown in the app before any purchase.

Do I need an account?

Your list and reminders work without one, on your phone only. Plans, AI, friends, chats and the cloud copy need an account.

Do guests need the app?

No. The organizers send guests a link, and guests answer there whether they're coming, with no app and no sign-up. The organizers manage the guest list.

Is there an iPhone version?

Lahgog is available for Android for now.

What does the AI see?

Only what the request needs, and only when you ask. The app explains what is sent before the first use. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is the app in English?

Yes. The app is Hebrew first, and you can switch it to English in Settings.
